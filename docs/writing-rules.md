# Writing rules

These rules apply to everything in the project: comments, messages the
software prints, example settings and all documentation. Future changes must
follow them. `test/writingRules.test.ts` checks the mechanical parts.

1. No em dashes and no en dashes, anywhere.
2. No hyphen used as punctuation or as decoration. That means no hyphen
   standing between two phrases, and no row of hyphens drawn around or under a
   heading or between sections.
3. A hyphen is allowed only where it joins words into one term, such as
   "vendor-agnostic" or "speaker-attributed".

Reword the sentence instead. A comma, a colon, a full stop or brackets almost
always works.

Not covered by the rules, because they are syntax and not prose: arithmetic,
command options, arrows, and the markers a document format itself requires for
lists, tables and rules. The licence notice in `LICENSE` is fixed text and is
not edited for style.
